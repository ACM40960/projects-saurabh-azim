ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is MediGuide?

MediGuide is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation application designed to make medical information easier to explore through natural-language questions.

Medical Knowledge Base

Medical PDF content is processed and transformed into searchable vector representations.

Semantic Retrieval

Questions are compared semantically with medical text to identify the most relevant information.

Context-Grounded AI

GPT-4o receives retrieved medical context before producing a concise answer.

Natural Interaction

Users can explore medical information through a simple conversational interface.

SYSTEM WORKFLOW

How MediGuide Works

The complete pipeline transforms raw medical documents into retrievable knowledge for context-aware question answering.

01

Medical PDF

Medical documents are loaded from the project knowledge repository.

02

Text Chunking

Content is cleaned and divided into smaller overlapping sections.

03

Embeddings

Sentence Transformers convert each chunk into a semantic vector.

04

Pinecone

Embeddings are indexed for high-speed semantic similarity search.

05

GPT-4o

Retrieved context is combined with the question to generate the final answer.

RAG ARCHITECTURE

Retrieval before generation.

MediGuide does not simply send a question directly to a language model. It first searches the medical vector database for relevant context.

  • Query transformed into an embedding
  • Pinecone performs cosine similarity search
  • Top three relevant chunks are retrieved
  • Retrieved context is added to the system prompt
  • GPT-4o generates the final response
